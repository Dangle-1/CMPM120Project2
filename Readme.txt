This is a space themed arcade shooter game where the player has to shoot down incoming enemy ships with his own ship using lasers while simultaneously avoiding their lasers. Waves get increasingly difficult as the game progresses. The arrow keys and keys “A” and “D” move your ship left and right, the spacebar is used to fire lasers, and “R” restarts the game after the player loses. There are two types of enemies, one that moves downwards in a straight line, and another that moves in a zigzag downwards and is faster. The regular enemy is worth 10 points upon defeating, and the fast enemy is 20 points.
